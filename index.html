Yi's messaging rule (2026-10-21)

Keep it short. Focus on the model and its offensive capabilities. Include the evals. Say it plugs into any harness or agent.

Own the model: say "we built" and "we tasked it", never "according to the manufacturer". No AI slop. Match the X voice: evidence first, numbers front-loaded, punchy. See the flagship post: "We tasked OrcaCyber Zero 1.5 with investigating the critical PHP RCE vulnerabilities. It autonomously built a complete working proof-of-concept (PoC)."
